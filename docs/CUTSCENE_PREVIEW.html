v53: Native inclusion of mixing detail normals

Raw MAT flag 0x4 involves mixing normals, 0x08000000 is the contribution of the second mask. For suitable materials with two detail cards, the sum of the included masks selects one card by sign, and the weight is equal to the square of a limited amount. The previous sequential processing could completely replace the normal second card due to the missing mask. The correction uses a common CPU/GPU sample signedr and saves the animation of the coefficients according to the properties of M3A. In six scenes tested, this path affects six materials of Davis hair in SMX3_Cutene detail, the originals involved with the open mix of 202.

v52: UV normal/gloss and reflection blur

For conventional native class0 materials with UV0/1/9/10, the restored type4 matrix is used: rotation of XYZ around the center 0.5, negative offset and tiling XY. The path covers the main color, normal/gloss, emissive, mask and alpha layers, including explicit UVs for RTT; the matrix is used before the framebuffer coordinates are revolutionized. The already mixed values of the properties of M3A are taken. Two lines are transmitted to the GPU via mat4x2. Height/parallax, flipbook, special choice of the owner of UV detail, other sources of normal/PBR remain on the previous path.

For a suitable cubic Envio with a raw MAT flag 0x800 connected Windows formula SC2: max(0, 0.5*log2(max(dot(dx,dx),dot(dy,dy)))) + floor(log2(sourceSize))*(1-glossAlpha)Derivatives are taken from the UV normal map, or from the interpolated eye-to-vertex multiplied by 0.25. Gloss alpha is counted after layer operations, before squared, and without additional clamp; its value is used repeatedly for specular and local lights. Bias depends on the original size of the DDS, not on the number of available mip levels. EnvioMask stores implicit LOD. For zero derivatives, a limit is given mip0, so as not to cause an indefinite in GLSL. log2(0)The status of the native sampler and the matching of the actual frames have not yet been confirmed.

v51: initial mip levels of cubemap

For ordinary material with a single owner of the environment matrix and UV2/7, the DDS faces are transmitted to the present samplerCube BC1/BC2/BC3 blocks are loaded directly through S3TC; without suitable support, each source layer is decoded in RGBA. The chain is not generated from a 2D cross; the last available layer is explicitly defined. Pre-blurred single-level DDSs are stored as they were in the resources. The atlas remains for the rest of the paths. Native formula explicit LOD glossiness, multiple owners of the matrix alternate and runtime mode are not yet connected; matching with SC2 frames is not performed.

For the new cubemap path, linear/trilinear and clamp are chosen. These are the preview settings: the exact sampler state in each passage of SC2 has not yet been restored. The presence of the original mip levels does not by itself confirm a match in lighting.

v50: Orientation of DDS cubemap faces

When packing the DDS cubemap into a two-dimensional horizontal cross, the strings unfold inside each facet: this compensates for the reverse vertical axis of the existing cubeUv. Example of Direct3D and OpenGL. Conventional 2D DDS don't deploy; only the cubemap cache gets a new version. The original DDS remains unchanged. The original mip levels, roughness/LOD and face-bound filtering still need to be moved, the frame comparison with SC2 has not been done.

Real ambient. samplerCube The check of distinguishable gradient faces covers 96 directions separately for the materials atlas and ambient cubemap; the installed ambient light v49 is saved byte to byte.

v49: UV reflection matrix

For conventional standard M3 materials with one active reflection layer, offset, XYZ rotation, and scale are applied to the world normal and direction of view prior to reflection calculation. The order is verified by limited native-function reproduction; the existing mixing of M3A channels for each property is used. The reflection no longer receives repeated UV transformation after projection and parallax offset. Several environment matrix owners, flipbook, special/FX and alternate modes retain the same path; and matching the original roughness/LOD frames is not yet confirmed.

v48: Reflection vector and normal map

For standard native M3 reflection materials, envi/enviMask uses a normal after normal map in the world and an interpolated eye-to-top vector. The camera space geometry normal was previously used. Programs warm up in advance and the route is included in the shader cache key. 453 pairs of shaders are checked offline by the Khronos glslang compiler, including 176 options for the new route. The full order of the UV environment matrix, roughness/LOD, two-sided surfaces and real GPU pixels still need to be checked.

v47: LotV materials and lost textures

Fixed the polarity of the native-flag 0x800 to reduce glare, including old built-in models. Added RGB32 DDS cubemap: four maps of reflections of the LotV bridge were previously discarded by the decoder. Texture search now takes into account the selected locale and order of dependencies: the textures of Raynor's body and Hyperion screens are in enUS.SC2Assets/ru.SC2Assets. Original game files are saved. Blurrowing reflections by roughness, real GPU frames and matching with the original are not yet confirmed.

v46: Texture filtering from ModKit

Static textures of materials use anisotropic filtering up to 8× with a graphics card restriction. Without extension, trilinear mipmaps are preserved. RTT, video, ambient cube, bone palettes and post-processing retain their settings. The selected coefficient returns in frame diagnosis and MCP as textureAnisotropy. MIT authorship Modkit contributors is retained. This is a preview quality setting; the value of the native sampler SC2 and visual improvement on the GPU are not yet confirmed.

v45: WebGL frame via MCP and light budget

cutscene.render creates a local player and receives a PNG from its WebGL2 canvas through the background Edge/Chrome. The cutscene does not open in the SC2 Editor. The frame uses the current player's materials, light, effects and RTT; no match to the original engine is claimed. The controller and protocol are verified without starting the browser; end-to-end verification of real capture has not yet been performed.

v44: Sound, bookmarks and light of materials

The scale and rewind cover the scene to the last End Scene on, keeping the original long service tracks in the data. Current time is shown with full length; end mark is accurate. The bookmarks are accessible by mouse and keyboard, transitions take into account filters and reset the old sound clock. For 2D gain, channels and predefined SoundData levels are saved; the initial one is applied before the source starts.

The local light is summed up before the reflections are superimposed; the specular reflections depend on the incident light separately from the specular texture of the material. The list of modes has "Materials · Without LUT" to check for the effects of color correction. This is a diagnostic comparison: the normal mode uses color correction from the data. Native mixer/ducking/reverb, shadows and matching of SC2 frames are not yet confirmed. Controller checks and WebGL command records do not replace visual comparison with GPUs.

Nova MP02_010 built-in sound check: all 12 downloaded resources are used in 2D. Nine events after a given amplification have countdowns above 1.0 inside their time windows (maximum 1,565); fades and volume curves are absent for these events. This measurement before summation and output, not the recording of the browser or the original SC2. Possible overload and balance of voices with music remain open until group mixing, downmix and engine volume settings are restored. Author levels are not normalized individually.

Accuracy and limitations

This is a local renderer, not a full SC2 engine. There are no terrain, dynamic shadows, precise engine DOF/HDR/lighting, screen distortions, terrain collision and complete projector semantics. Not all material families have an exact native equivalent — the original runtime produces diagnostics. Not all particle tail/model orientations, random lifecycle and ribbon variants are reproduced in full. Bone masks, IK/billboards and partial inheritance still require implementation and reconciliation. Unknown numerics get + diagnostics. Scenes without bin keys can remain in a curved/Videal game.

The PNG/contact sheet computes surface material with a CPU rasterizer with the same pose/time/camera, but not pixel-identical WebGL: approximate lighting/UV/reflection/face normals and no live particles/ribbons. For an accurate shot of the current WebGL, use Save Frame in the player. On a complex scene, the CPU PNG is much slower than playback; for fast iterations snapshots=false.
