Savings

Measured o200k_base: facial_info response with one animation - 300 tokens; two verification operations - 121 tokens. Preview snapshots=false - 4.671 s. Geometry, sound and FXA curves remain in the local file, do not return the entire AI. This is the amount of payload; real consumption of model tokens and billing are not available.

Why not 100%?

FXA blink/head/eye and M3 emotion bindings, ConversationLink/driver/import/subtitles, MasterSound, native mixer/envelopes, links/references and full HDR/postprocess still require implementation and comparison. Unsupported graphs and channels are diagnosed rather than replaced by volume animation.

Source of format/process: SC2 FaceFX handbook submitted; FaceFX: Types of Graph NodesPublic definitions of Legendary Explorer fields were used to reconcile the structure; SDK code is not included, third-party code is not copied.
