2. What's already been implemented

Lighting:
- General module src/modules/lighting: reading LightData, presets, inheritance
  catalogs, local redefinitions, state of light, and general computations.
- Ambient and Key/Fill/Back, environment cube and independent card multiplier
  environment; author's ambientEnvironmentMap in the cutscene.
- Local point/spotlight sources, attenuation by distance,
  The confirmed shape of the cone, the transformation of position and direction.
- Read and Animate M3 LITE Built Sources
- Animation EnvironmentLight/ActiveLight, numerical transitions between states
  Some rules for selecting discrete parameters are still a preview policy.
- Confirmed HDR multipliers of conventional illuminated standard materials.
  Unshaded, special families and alternative terrain-HDR not issued
  for a fully restored general case.
- Tonal operators, ColorizationContrast communication with tonal contrast
  Transformation, HDR WhiteShift.
- HSL/levels/gamma/Colorize, ordered Variations and color table 16x16x16.
  For conventional HDR-path confirmed linear filtration, clamp on the axes
  and enable colorMap by default.
- Separate knots of the cutscene fog, their time windows and parameter animation;
  Without active Fog, the default density is zero.

Materials, animations and cameras:
- Typed M3/M3A channels, native animation ID and additional communication
  animations with properties of materials, cameras and light sources.
- Sampling each material property from available layers instead of
  Select only one clip; support for STC/STS and part of the native competition.
  Complete mixing of all layer varieties has not yet been confirmed.
- UV transformations, alpha, color, emission and composite passages of materials.
- Correcting the depth of combined passages and sorting transparent objects.
- RTT/video_channel, coordinate texture and source camera.
- Fixes Actor/Light transformations, camera base and near/far clip.
  CPU geometry check Raynor shows front framing;
  The final match of the textured GPU image has not yet been verified.
- Particles/ribbons, sound, subtitles and part of facial animation as preview functions.

Interface and productivity:
- Blue interface, Director, object tree, Property/Value on the right
  Preservation of original properties and display of current values.
- Selection of the object by timeline tracks, transport, bookmarks and End Scene.
- 16:9/16:10 frame formats and individual white guides.
- Load percentage; 100% is achieved after the first frame drawn.
- Manual movement of the camera starts with the last calculated camera.
- Preliminary shader training, reuse of GPU geometry
  and postures, caching properties of materials and cutting off invisible objects.
  There is no 60 FPS guarantee on all scenes and devices.

MCP and editing:
- lighting.inspect reads the general light from the installed game and local
  directories; the team itself does not change anything.
- terrain.lighting.inspect / terrain.lighting.plan and terrain operations
  allow you to prepare and save the settings / purpose of the LightData preset.
- cutscene operations allow you to change supported nodes Light, Fog,
  EnvironmentLight and their properties with subsequent preservation/preview.
- M3 LITE supports reading/animation/preview.
  The light inside the M3 itself has not yet been realized.

3. What is not yet or what is not confirmed

- There is no full native shadow passage in the installed render yet.
  The studied formulas and shadow bindings do not mean their introduction.
- It does not match the original DeferredLight/frame graph SC2.
  All local/transparent shadows and all sorting/passing options.
- WebGL's Bloom, DOF and AO remain approximate. Full native SSAOs.
  Bloom extraction/blur/composition and optics have not yet been restored.
- Not closed all formats render targets, color conversions
  HDR-bypass/overlay and other alternative paths.
- Not fully implemented reflections, distortion, depth blend, volumetric
  Special families of materials/effects.
- The exact RGB/HDR ambient path without the environment cube remains partial.
- Interpolation of discrete presets/environmental maps and some complex cases
  Inheritance/transitions use a clearly limited preview policy.
- CPU cutscene shots don't have full local lighting and post-processing.
  PNG and WebGL can not be considered the same in coverage.
- There is no complete terrain/map-context render inside the cutscene.
- Not confirmed all rules of bone masks, IK, additive, whole/partial,
  fades and all families of facial/material animation.
- Accurate matching of all cameras, RTT, lighting and final frames with the editor
  1:1 has not yet been confirmed. Editor and the game for this test have not been launched.

4. What more needs to be added on lighting - on priority

Priority 1: Shadows from the main directional light Key.
You need to complete the selection of the shadow camera area by camera and objects.
identify active bias/culling and depth formats, trace native flags
Then add a real depth passage and a shadow reception.
Key diffuse/specular, retaining independent ambient and Fill/Back.
After that, check the self-shading, map boundaries, softness and animation.

Priority 2: Local and color transparent shadows.
Save shadowcasting and source identity in runtime; implement
Confirmed spot/local shadow maps, separate depth/color maps
Transparent shadows, model/material flags and quality/budget constraints.

Priority 3: Full HDR and bloom.
Restore the present order of brightness, downscale, blur and addition,
its parameters/quality, buffer formats and alternative HDR passes.
Don’t pick up an arbitrary general gamma/brightness for a single screenshot.

Priority 4: Ambient, AO, and Reflections.
Close the remaining ambient RGB/HDR fallback, SSAO and reflection sources.
They're aligned with normals and families of materials. There's a color LUT.
But it doesn't replace the missing shadows, the reflections and the right lighting.

Priority 5: fog, DOF and special passageways.
Bring the integration of fog with transparent objects and map context, restore
native DOF, distortion/depth blend, and the impact of quality on pass selection.

Priority 6: Comparison and Configuration through MCP.
Check the same scenes, time, camera, aspect and graphics settings
And then expand the supported light settings on the saved originals.
in MCP without promising features that don’t work in the render itself.
